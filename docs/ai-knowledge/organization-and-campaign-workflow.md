# Organization & Campaign Workflow

This workflow governs large-scale fundraising efforts by registered entities.

## Organization Registration
1. An organization registers with their details, Official Email, and a Registration License/Document.
2. The City Admin reviews the documents.
3. Once approved by the City Admin, the organization becomes `VERIFIED` and can create campaigns.

## Campaign Lifecycle
1. **Creation**: A verified organization creates a campaign with a large goal amount, specific category, and a deadline.
2. **Review**: The campaign enters `PENDING_REVIEW` and is routed to the City Admin.
3. **Approval**: The City Admin reviews the campaign and can Approve, Reject, or Request Changes.
4. **Publication**: Once approved, the campaign is published to the public board.
5. **Completion**: When the campaign reaches its goal amount, it is automatically marked as `COMPLETED`. Or, if it reaches its deadline, it is closed. Organizations must upload a "Success Photo URL" to prove the impact of the completed campaign.
